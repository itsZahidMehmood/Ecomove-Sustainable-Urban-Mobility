EcoMove – Sustainable Urban Mobility Analytics & Interactive Dashboard
Data Science | Python | Data Visualisation | Exploratory Data Analysis | Plotly Dash

📌 Project Overview

EcoMove is a data-driven urban mobility analytics project designed to investigate public transportation performance across 12 European cities in six countries.

The project uses Python-based data analysis and interactive visualisation techniques to explore passenger demand, service reliability, weather-related delays, customer satisfaction, financial performance, accessibility and environmental impact.

An interactive dashboard was developed using Plotly Dash, allowing users to explore transportation performance through multiple filters and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The main objective is to transform raw transportation data into meaningful insights that can support better operational planning and evidence-based decision-making.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyse passenger demand and transportation trends over time.

Compare transportation performance across cities and transport modes.

Investigate relationships between service delays and customer satisfaction.

Evaluate the impact of weather conditions on transportation reliability.

Examine financial performance and cost recovery across transport modes.

Analyse environmental performance through CO₂ savings.

Investigate the operational impact of special event days.

Develop an interactive dashboard for exploring transportation performance.

Important insights:

Metro accounted for approximately 63% of total passenger demand.

Paris and Berlin represented approximately 22% of network demand.

Bus services showed weaker reliability compared with other major transport modes.

Each additional minute of delay was associated with approximately 0.14 points lower customer satisfaction.

Snow and rain conditions were associated with increased transportation delays.

Event days increased passenger demand but also negatively affected service reliability.

Micro-mobility services showed significant financial losses despite their environmental and accessibility contributions.
